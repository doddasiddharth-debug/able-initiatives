Mentorship is free for students. It isn't free to run.

ABLE is entirely volunteer-led, so your donation goes further than most: there's no staff to pay. It goes straight into keeping every program free for the next student who signs up.

Where your donation goes

We run on a small, predictable budget. Here's what it covers.

1

Test prep & program materials

Practice tests, study guides, and first-aid training supplies for ABLE Preps and ABLE Health sessions.

2

Workshops & hosting costs

Venue, platform, and materials costs for in-person and virtual workshops across all three branches.

3

Reaching more schools

Outreach so students outside our founding school hear that these programs exist and are free.
