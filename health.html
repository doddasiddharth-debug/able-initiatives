ABLE Health

Health literacy, made accessible.

ABLE Health organizes free webinars and workshops that teach practical health knowledge and open a window into healthcare careers, for students who might never get that exposure otherwise.

Join ABLE Health See all programs

What we do

Webinars and workshops that school skips over.

A lot of essential health knowledge, how to read a nutrition label, what to do in an emergency, how the healthcare system actually works, never makes it into a classroom. ABLE Health runs sessions that fill those gaps in plain language.

We also help students who are curious about medicine, nursing, or public health see what those paths really look like, straight from people already on them.

What we run

Webinars, workshops, and speaker panels

Health literacy workshops

Hands-on sessions on nutrition, mental health, and navigating care, the everyday knowledge that keeps people healthy.

First-aid & safety training

Practical basics like first aid and CPR-adjacent skills, so students know what to do when it counts.

Healthcare career webinars

Guest speakers and panels on paths into medicine, nursing, and public health, plus guidance sessions for pre-health students.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to understand their health, or build a career in it.

You don't need to be pre-med to join. ABLE Health is for any student who wants to be more informed about their own wellbeing, and for those thinking seriously about a future in healthcare.
