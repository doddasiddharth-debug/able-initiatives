About us

Started by students who noticed a gap, and decided to close it.

ABLE Initiatives is a student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We make educational resources free and open to every student across college prep, health, and business, because the things that change outcomes shouldn't be locked behind a paywall.

Why we exist

Ability is everywhere. Access isn't.

ABLE started with a simple observation: the students who get furthest aren't always the most capable: they're the ones who happened to know someone who could point them in the right direction. A structured test-prep plan. A session that explained how applications actually work. Someone who had already walked the path and was willing to explain it.

Those things are usually available, for a price. We wanted to make them available without one.

What we believe

The principles behind how we run ABLE

$0

Free, no exceptions

No tuition, no application fee, no premium tier. Every program stays free for every student, every time.

ALL

Open to everyone

No application, no eligibility screen, no waitlist. If you want to be in the room, you're in.

SL

Student-led

Run by high schoolers and college students volunteering alongside their own classes, not a top-down institution.
