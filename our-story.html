Our story

Why ABLE exists.

ABLE Initiatives makes educational resources free and open to every student: workshops, webinars, and guest speaker sessions across college prep, health, and business, with no fee and nothing to qualify for.

What we do

Put the resources in the room, and take the price off.

Everything ABLE runs comes back to the same idea. Workshops, webinars and guest speaker sessions across college prep, health and business — the study plans, the practice, the person who has already walked the path and will explain it to you.

Those things exist almost everywhere. They're just usually available for a price. We wanted them available without one: no fee, no application, nothing to qualify for, and the materials are yours to keep afterwards.

What we believe

The principles behind how we run ABLE

$0

Free, no exceptions

No tuition, no application fee, no premium tier. Every program stays free for every student, every time.

ALL

Open to everyone

No application, no eligibility screen, no waitlist. If you want to be in the room, you're in.

SL

Student-led

Run by high schoolers and college students volunteering alongside their own classes, not a top-down institution.

Want to see who's behind it?

ABLE is run entirely by students volunteering alongside their own classes.
