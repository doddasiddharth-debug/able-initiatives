ABLE Preps

SAT prep and college advice, for free.

ABLE Preps helps students get ready for the SAT and ACT and navigate the college application process, guided by students who went through it recently enough to remember exactly what was confusing.

Join ABLE Preps See all programs

What we do

Test prep and admissions guidance, minus the price tag.

Private tutoring and college consultants work, but they cost more than most families can spend. ABLE Preps gives students the same core support for free: a study plan, real practice, and live sessions where the questions without obvious answers actually get answered.

It's not a video library you watch alone. The sessions are live, so you can ask the questions you actually have.

Led by Helen Wan, President of ABLE Preps

What's included

Everything you need to apply with confidence

SAT & ACT prep

A study plan built around your timeline and target score, full-length practice tests, and review sessions that explain the why, not just the right answer.

College advice

Building a balanced school list, understanding deadlines and financial aid, and figuring out what actually matters in an application.

Essays & applications

Brainstorming, drafting, and honest feedback on personal statements and supplements, from volunteers who have read a lot of them.

Who it's for

Any student getting ready for what's next.

Whether you're just starting to study for the SAT, staring down application deadlines, or not sure where to begin, ABLE Preps meets you where you are, at a pace that works for you.
