Know how the law works.

Our newest branch. ABLE Law runs free workshops and webinars that introduce students to the law: how the legal system works, what it means for everyday life, and what a career in law looks like.

Join ABLE Law Meet the team

The law shapes everyday life. Most students never get to look inside it.

Laws decide what happens at school, at work, online and in court, but few students get the chance to learn how the system actually runs before they have to choose a path. ABLE Law gives them that chance, in plain language.

Like every ABLE branch, all of it is free, and open to anyone who turns up.
