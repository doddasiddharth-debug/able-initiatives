ABLE Engineering

How things get built.

Our newest branch. ABLE Engineering runs free workshops and webinars that introduce students to engineering: how things are designed, built, tested, and improved.

Join ABLE Engineering See all programs

What we do

Workshops and webinars on real engineering.

Most students never get to try engineering before they have to decide whether to study it. ABLE Engineering closes that gap with hands-on sessions and honest conversations about what engineers actually do all day.

Like every ABLE branch, all of it is free: no fee, no application, and nothing to qualify for.

EN

Led by Luke Dulski, President of ABLE Engineering

What we cover

Design, build, and the people who do it

Hands-on build sessions

Design-and-build workshops where you make something, test it, and learn why it worked or didn't.

How-it-works webinars

The engineering behind everyday things, explained from the ground up: no prerequisites assumed.

Guest engineers

People who build things for a living, on what the work is really like and how they got there.

Who it's for

Anyone who likes taking things apart, or wants to know how they go together.

You don't need robotics experience or a physics class under your belt. ABLE Engineering is for any student curious about how the built world works.
