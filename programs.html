Programs

Four branches. All volunteer-led. All free.

Pick the one that fits where you're headed, or reach out if you're not sure. Every branch runs on the same model: free workshops, webinars, and materials, open to any student who wants them.

PR College Prep

ABLE Preps

Study plans, practice tests, and application support from students who've been through the process recently, recently enough to remember exactly what was confusing about it.

  • Personalized study plans built around your timeline and target score
  • Practice tests with real feedback, not just an answer key
  • Essay review and college application strategy
  • Sessions run by students who took the same test within the last few years
Learn more about ABLE Preps Join
PR

Who it's for

Students prepping for the SAT/ACT or working through college applications, who want a study plan and a real person checking in, not another subscription.

Led by

Helen Wan, President of ABLE Preps

HE Healthcare

ABLE Health

Health literacy sessions, first-aid training, and speaker panels for students considering medicine, nursing, or public health, from people already on that path.

  • Health literacy workshops covering topics that rarely get taught in school
  • Hands-on first-aid and CPR-adjacent training sessions
  • Sessions on pre-med, nursing, and public health tracks
  • Guidance on shadowing, volunteering, and building a healthcare-ready résumé
Learn more about ABLE Health Join
HE

Who it's for

Students curious about medicine, nursing, or public health who want practical exposure and to hear from people further along the same track.

Led by

Eric Huang, President of ABLE Health

BU Business

ABLE Business

Budgeting and investing basics, an entrepreneurship track, and workshops for students curious about business, whether that means a startup or a first internship.

  • Personal finance fundamentals: budgeting, saving, and investing basics
  • An entrepreneurship track for students building their own projects
  • Sessions on internships, resumes, and case interviews
  • Guest workshops from professionals working across different industries
Learn more about ABLE Business Join
BU

Who it's for

Students curious about business, finance, or entrepreneurship who want practical skills and to learn from people who've worked in the field.

Led by

Rhythm Kasat, President of ABLE Business

EN Engineering

ABLE Engineering

Our newest branch: hands-on workshops and webinars that introduce students to engineering — how things are designed, built, tested, and improved.

  • Design-and-build workshop sessions
  • Webinars on the engineering behind everyday things
  • Guest sessions with people who build things for a living
Learn more about ABLE Engineering Join
EN

Who it's for

Students curious about how the built world works — no robotics experience or physics class required.

Led by

Luke Dulski, President of ABLE Engineering
