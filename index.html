Where you start shouldn't decide how far you get.

Advancing Better Learning Empowerment

ABLE Initiatives makes educational resources free and open to every student. We run workshops, webinars, and guest speaker sessions across college prep, health, and business, so that learning doesn't come down to what your family can afford.

Every program is 100% free: no fees, no exceptions.

Why this matters

Education is still out of reach for most of the world.

Not because students aren't capable, but because the resources that make learning possible were never handed out evenly.

273M

children and youth are out of school worldwide.

UNESCO
70%

of 10-year-olds in low- and middle-income countries can't read and understand a simple story.

World Bank & UNICEF, 2022
739M

young people and adults lack basic literacy skills, two thirds of them women.

UNESCO

The gap isn't only overseas. In the classrooms we sit in, the students who get furthest are often the ones who happened to have access to a study plan, a practice test, or someone who had already done it. Nearly everywhere, those things carry a price. We don't think they should.

Our mission

Free educational resources, for every student.

ABLE Initiatives exists to put learning materials, workshops, and expert sessions in front of any student who wants them, at no cost and with nothing to qualify for.

We run webinars and in-person workshops across three branches, bring in speakers who have done the work, and share the materials afterwards so they outlast the session. No fee, no application, no cap on who can come.

How it works

02

03

Join the workshop or webinar, then take the slides and resources with you afterwards.
